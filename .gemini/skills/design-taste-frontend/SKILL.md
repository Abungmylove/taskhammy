# tasteskill: Taskhammy — Academic Tutoring Landing Page

## 0. BRIEF INFERENCE
Reading this as: **cute/kawaii consumer landing page** for a women-owned Indonesian academic tutoring service (bimbingan tugas & skripsi), with a playful hamster mascot. Targeting students (SMA/Kuliah) aged 16-25.

Vibe words: **cute, warm, soft, playful, imut, cozy, feminin, friendly, trustworthy, warm-craft**

## 1. THE THREE DIALS
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7` (Fun rounded asymmetric cards, wavy section dividers, playful spacing)
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 5` (Soft fade-in animations, gentle bounce hover effects)
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 4` (Airy layout, clear hierarchy, enough whitespace for friendly readability)

## 2. ANTI-DEFAULT DISCIPLINE
This is NOT a SaaS app. Avoid: dark zinc backgrounds, emerald green accents, mono fonts, terminal aesthetics, glass morphism, purple gradients.

## 3. DESIGN DIRECTIVES

### Color Palette
- **Background**: Warm cream / off-white `#FDF6EE` or `#FFF9F3`
- **Primary Accent**: Warm brown / latte `#A0785A` or `#9E7256`
- **Secondary Accent**: Soft peach / blush pink `#F4A69A` or `#F2A191`
- **Text**: Dark warm brown `#3D2B1F` for headings, `#6B4E3D` for body
- **Cards/Surface**: Creamy warm white `#FAF0E6` with soft shadow

### Typography
- **Headings**: Rounded, playful font — Nunito, Poppins, or Plus Jakarta Sans (rounded weight)
- **Body**: Poppins Regular, warm and readable
- **Accent Text**: Bubbly mono-free style, no JetBrains Mono

### Visual Style
- Rounded corners everywhere (radius 16-24px)
- Soft drop shadows with warm tint `rgba(160,120,90,0.12)`
- Subtle polka-dot or paw-print texture patterns on section backgrounds
- Hamster emoji/mascot appears decoratively throughout

### Language
- **Indonesian language** throughout the entire landing page
- Friendly "kamu" tone, not formal
